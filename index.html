Our History

A Legacy of Excellence

Founded in 2008, London College Nansana has grown from a small community school to one of the most respected educational institutions in the region.

🏗️ 2008 - Foundation

London College Nansana was established with just 50 students and 5 teachers, driven by a vision to provide quality education to the community.

📈 2012 - Growth

Expanded facilities and programs, introducing science laboratories and computer labs. Student population grew to 200.

🏆 2018 - Recognition

Achieved outstanding academic results and received recognition as one of the top schools in the district.

🎓 2024 - Excellence

Today, we serve over 500 students with 50+ qualified teachers, maintaining our commitment to educational excellence.

Admission Process

How to Join London College Nansana

  1. Fill out the application form online or at the school office.
  2. Submit required documents (see Fees & Requirements).
  3. Attend an interview and assessment (if applicable).
  4. Receive admission decision and pay registration fee.
  5. Complete enrollment and join orientation.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What is the medium of instruction at London College Nansana?

A1: The medium of instruction is English. We provide additional language support for non-native speakers.

Q2: How can I apply for admission?

A2: You can apply online through our website or download the application form and submit it to the school office.

Q3: What are the school fees?

A3: Please refer to the Fees & Requirements section for detailed information on the fee structure.

Q4: Does the school offer scholarships?

A4: Yes, we offer merit-based and need-based scholarships. Please check the Scholarships section for more information.

Q5: What extracurricular activities are available?
